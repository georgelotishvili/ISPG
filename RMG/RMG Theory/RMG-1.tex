\section{Pass/Fail Gates}

\begin{gate}[Core pass]
RMG passes as a core framework if it derives:
\[
S_{\rm EH}+S_{\rm matter}
\]
as the low-energy fixed point of $\mathcal M$, with positive physical
Hamiltonian, universal metric coupling, and only two vacuum tensor
gravitational degrees of freedom.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Core fail]
RMG fails if the base-medium produces an observable local preferred frame,
species-dependent metric coupling, extra scalar/vector radiation, wrong
gravitational-wave speed, negative-energy physical modes, or non-closing ADM
constraints.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[GW detection fail]
RMG fails in the tested local gravitational-wave sector if observed
interferometer signals are explained only by a pure common-mode scalar
breathing that should be self-calibrated away, if an unsuppressed
breathing/scalar polarization survives local polarization tests, or if no
protected differential TT/shear record signal is produced.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Tensor-closure fail]
RMG fails in the tested local tensor sector if the candidate record dynamics
does not produce exactly two positive, luminal, common-cone TT modes, or if it
leaves an unsuppressed scalar/vector, massive spin-2, negative-energy, or
extra shear radiation channel.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Preferred-frame fail]
RMG fails in the tested local core if an independent $u^\mu_{\mathcal M}$ enters
local operators, or if lapse/shift acquire low-energy kinetic terms.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Factorization fail]
RMG fails in the tested local core if physical low-energy principal operators do
not factorize through one $G^{\mu\nu}$, or if the failure matrix
$B_{AB}^{\mu\nu}$ produces species-dependent cones, a preferred-frame tensor, or
a geometry cone different from the matter cone.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Tail-projection fail]
RMG fails in the tested local core if stable low-energy particles have different
gravitational projection ratios $q_A/(E_A/c^2)$, or if their long tails require
different long-range spacetime carriers.
\end{gate}

\begin{gate}[Medium-residue fail]
RMG fails in the tested local core if $\Delta_{\mathcal M}$ leaves an
unsuppressed preferred-frame term, extra operational tensor, non-ADM constraint
bracket, or low-energy scalar/vector propagating degree of freedom.

\begin{gate}[MOND-window fail]

solar-system, binary-pulsar or gravitational-wave local tests, or if it changes
\(\gamma=1\), \(\beta=1\), \(c_{\rm GW}=c\), universal free fall, or the
two-tensor-mode vacuum sector.
\end{gate}
